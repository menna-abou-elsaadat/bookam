🏠 Unit Rental Management System
This application helps property owners manage their rental units — whether studios, apartments, or other spaces. It provides a complete toolkit for tracking unit details, financials, and performance, making rental management simple and transparent.

✨ Features
Unit Details  
Store and organize information about each unit (rooms, space, installments, ownership share percentage).

Expense Tracking  
Record and categorize expenses such as utilities, maintenance, and services.

Revenue Management  
Track rental income and other revenues for each unit.

Profit Calculation  
Automatically calculate net profit by comparing revenues against expenses.

Installments & Shares  
Manage unit installments and ownership share percentages for partners or investors.

Comprehensive Reporting  
Generate insights on expenses, revenues, and profitability to support smarter decisions.

🎯 Purpose
This app centralizes everything about unit renting and financial management in one place. Property owners can:

Save time by automating calculations.

Gain clarity on unit performance.

Improve profitability through accurate tracking.
